Privacy Policy

Oura Sleep Analysis is a private, non-commercial application developed for the personal use of its owner.

Data Collection

The application accesses Oura Ring data only with the account owner's authorization. This may include sleep, heart rate, readiness, activity, and related health measurements.

Data Storage

Downloaded data is stored locally on the owner's personal computer.

Data Sharing

The application does not automatically transmit collected health data to third parties or make it publicly available. The owner may independently choose to share exported data for analysis.

Data Retention

The owner controls the locally stored data and may delete it at any time.

Security

Access to Oura data uses Oura's authorization system. Application credentials and authorization tokens are not published on this website.
